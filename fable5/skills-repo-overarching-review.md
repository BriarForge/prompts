# Skills Repository Overarching Review & Improvement

**Category:** self-improvement  
**Tags:** [skills, governance, maintenance, discovery, hermes-prime]

```text
Objective: Conduct a comprehensive, overarching review of the entire KovaForge skills repository and produce a prioritized improvement plan that raises the overall quality, consistency, discoverability, and maintainability of the skill ecosystem.

Ground the review in this verified location:
- Skills repository root: /Users/mike/Projects/KovaForge/skills/

Key areas to assess and improve:
- Structural consistency of SKILL.md files (frontmatter completeness, required sections, verification checklists, pitfalls sections)
- Tagging quality and coverage across all skills
- Strength and completeness of related_skills relationships
- Identification of maintenance debt (stale, incomplete, low-signal, or outdated skills)
- Gaps in governance (versioning, authorship, required_environment_variables, required_commands)
- Duplication, fragmentation, or overlapping functionality between skills
- Overall discoverability and how well the current structure supports agents finding the right skills

Done looks like: 
1. A clear summary of current state across the repository
2. A prioritized list of concrete improvements (categorized by effort and impact)
3. Specific recommendations for governance rules or templates that would prevent future degradation
4. A shortlist of the highest-value individual skills that should be patched or rewritten first

Verification: The resulting plan should be actionable enough that executing the top 5–7 recommendations would produce a measurable lift in skill quality and reduce future manual maintenance effort.
```

## Notes
- Treat the skills repository as a critical shared asset.
- Focus on systemic improvements rather than fixing individual skills in isolation.
