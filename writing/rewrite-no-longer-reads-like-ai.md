# Rewrite so it no longer reads like AI

```text
Rewrite the text so it no longer reads like AI.

Break any overly controlled structure. Let the thinking move the way a real mind does: irregular in places, blunt in others, slower where it needs to land. Shake up stiff phrasing and formulaic wording so every sentence feels chosen in the moment, not generated.

Remove polish that makes it sound constructed, over-corrected, or too precise. It should sound like something a person said out loud without second-guessing every word.

Give it a real voice and point of view. Neutral observer tone is not allowed. Let opinions, small contradictions, and natural shifts in tone show up.

Add the micro-imperfections of someone who has actually lived the subject: a strong opinion here, a hesitation there, an incomplete sentence where it fits. Do not tidy those up afterward.

Read it as spoken language. Fix any sentence that would create an unnatural pause or break conversational rhythm.

Then identify the most artificial-sounding lines and rewrite them as if the author were talking to a close friend. No filter. No perfection.
```
